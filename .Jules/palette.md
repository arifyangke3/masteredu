## 2025-05-10 - Dynamic ARIA Labels on Toggle & Action Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (such as audio toggles or table row action icons) require dynamic, state-aware `aria-label` attributes and explicit `focus-visible` ring indicators so screen reader users and keyboard navigators can accurately discern action context.
**Action:** Always provide stateful `aria-label` and `focus-visible:ring-2` on icon-only interactive controls.
