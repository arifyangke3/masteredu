## 2025-05-18 - Semantic Selection Cards & Form Association in Educational Portals
**Learning:** In student/classroom portals where active games or quiz decks are listed as interactive cards, using `div` with `onClick` completely isolates keyboard and screen-reader students from selecting activities. Converting them into semantic `<button type="button">` with `aria-pressed`, `aria-label`, and `focus-visible:ring-2` provides full keyboard accessibility, visual focus clarity, and immediate screen reader context. Furthermore, explicitly linking labels via `htmlFor` and `id` ensures young learners and assistive technology users can reliably focus input fields by tapping or clicking labels.
**Action:** When implementing selectable cards or classroom dashboards, always use semantic `<button type="button">` with `text-left`, `w-full`, and explicit `aria-label` / `aria-pressed`, pairing every form input with matching `htmlFor` and `id`.
